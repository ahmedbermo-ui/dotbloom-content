Privacy Policy – Dotbloom

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Dotbloom itself does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-up and no analytics of its own. The small ad banner (see Ads) is shown by Google, which collects some data to do so.

What stays on your device

  • Your stickers, daily streak, settings and purchases are saved only on your device.
  • If your phone backs up to your Google account (an Android setting you control), Android may include Dotbloom's saved progress in that backup. The developer has no access to it.

Downloads

The app downloads the list of puzzle packs and the packs themselves from ahmedbermo-ui.github.io (GitHub Pages). These are plain file downloads: nothing about you or your progress is sent. Like any website, GitHub may briefly log the network address of the request; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Ads

Players who have not bought anything see one small banner at the top of the home screen and the sticker book, shown by Google AdMob. There are no ads while you play. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your approximate location from your network address, and how you interact with the ad. Ads are limited to content suitable for everyone.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google shows only limited, non-personalised ads until you agree; after your first three days of play the app asks once, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone's settings. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Any purchase removes the ads for good.

Purchases

Packs and Dotbloom Plus are bought through Google Play, behind a simple grown-up question. Payment details are handled by Google; the app only learns which products you own, so it can unlock them.

Voice and sound

When a picture is finished, the app can say its name using your phone's built-in text-to-speech. Nothing is recorded and the microphone is never used.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
  • It has no analytics code of its own, and the developer gets no data about you.
  • It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location permission.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it saved on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.

Contact

Use the developer contact details shown on the Dotbloom page in Google Play.
