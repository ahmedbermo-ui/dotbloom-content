Privacy Policy – My Dotbloom

Last updated: 26 October 2026

My Dotbloom is a dot-to-dot game for all ages, including children. The app itself does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-up and no analytics of its own. The small ad banner (see Ads) is shown by Google AdMob under its rules for apps used by children.

What stays on your device

  • Your stickers, daily streak, settings and purchases are saved only on your device.
  • If your phone backs up to your Google account (an Android setting you control), Android may include My Dotbloom's saved progress in that backup. The developer has no access to it.

Downloads

The app downloads the list of puzzle packs and the packs themselves from ahmedbermo-ui.github.io (GitHub Pages). These are plain file downloads: nothing about you or your progress is sent. Like any website, GitHub may briefly log the network address of the request; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Ads

Players who have not bought Dotbloom Plus see one small banner at the top of the screen, shown by Google AdMob and kept well away from the dots. Because the game is also played by children, every ad request is marked as directed at children: ads are not personalised, are limited to content rated suitable for everyone, and the app does not use the advertising ID (the permission is removed from the app). Google may still process limited technical data, such as the network address, to deliver and measure the ad and to prevent fraud. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Dotbloom Plus removes the ads for good.

Purchases

The only purchase is Dotbloom Plus (every pack and no ads), bought through Google Play behind a simple grown-up question. Payment details are handled by Google; the app only learns that Plus is owned, so it can unlock the packs.

Voice and sound

When a picture is finished, the app can say its name, from a recording that comes with the pack, or with your phone's built-in text-to-speech. Nothing is recorded and the microphone is never used.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
  • It has no analytics code of its own, and the developer gets no data about you.
  • It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location permission.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it saved on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.

Contact

Use the developer contact details shown on the My Dotbloom page in Google Play.
